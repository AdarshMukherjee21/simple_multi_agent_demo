New Employee Onboarding Guide

Week 1: IT Setup and Orientation

New employees receive their laptop and access credentials on day one. IT setup includes
email account creation, VPN access, and installation of required software such as Slack,
the internal project tracker, and the HR portal login.

Probation Period

All new hires undergo a 90-day probation period. Performance is reviewed at the 30-day,
60-day, and 90-day marks by the reporting manager. Confirmation of employment happens
after successful completion of the 90-day review.

Benefits Enrollment

Employees must enroll in health insurance and other benefits within the first 30 days
of joining. The benefits portal is accessible via the HR system using company credentials.
Dependents can be added to health insurance within the same 30-day window.

Reporting Structure

New employees are assigned a buddy from their team for the first 2 weeks to help with
onboarding questions. The reporting manager is responsible for setting 30-60-90 day goals
during the first week.
